When we are young, we think everything will stay the same forever. But as we grow up, we find that people leave, relationships end, interests change, and even we ourselves become different. The world never stops changing. What stays the same is only our wish to keep things as they are.

We often think that pain comes from losing something. However, the real reason is usually our attachment. We want every relationship to have a happy ending, every effort to bring success, and everything to happen as we expect.

But the world does not stop changing because of our wishes. The problem is not change itself. The problem is that we hope change will stop. So, going with the flow does not mean giving up. It means accepting that change is a natural part of life. We should work hard when we can and let go when we must. We should be thankful for what we have and calm when we lose something.

Perhaps the true meaning of going with the flow is not to let life make every choice for us, but to keep a peaceful mind while the world keeps changing.

Real freedom is not having everything happen the way we want. It is being able to live peacefully even when life does not go as planned.
