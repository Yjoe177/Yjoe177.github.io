As you grow older and move through one year after another, you start to realize how each year leaves its own mark on you. Some years become memories you wish you could relive, while others become lessons you wish you had never experienced.

Through my 19th year, I don’t know what to call it. Was it the worst year I have ever had, or was it one of the best? What I know for sure is that it was a year unlike any other. A year in which I experienced new moods, new emotions, and new versions of myself. Some moments took me beyond happiness, while others brought me wishing to die.

It is one of the few years that I can literally remember every chapter of, every period, every feeling. I learned a lot about myself, even though I still feel like I have so much more to understand. Maybe I am still learning, still discovering, and still a beginner when it comes to knowing who I truly am.

Goodbye to a year I will never, ever forget.
