If you mean school, then honestly, I can't get over them, and I think that's natural. But the solution is to deal with them and live with them.

School wasn't something easy to leave behind. You spent three years in a place where you spent more time than you did at home. And even though you suffered a lot in that place and went through many difficult times, you found people there who made things easier for you.

I still remember, to this day, the last day after we finished our projects in the second semester. We were all heading home, and we wouldn't be coming back again except before the exams. We were crying, and it was hard, but you eventually arrive at a phrase: "That's life."

The first time I heard that phrase was in my first year, when I met people in their third year whom I'd grown attached to. It was their last day, too, and I wasn't going to see them again. And then the days pass, and you find yourself saying it to those younger than you.

It's impossible to forget the memories.

---

I don’t know what this place holds that makes all these people so attached to it.

Our points of view differ; we argue and we discuss, yet what brings us together is our attachment to it.

Despite the suffering we have grown used to because of this place, it has always been the one that heals us.

I don’t know whether what heals us is the place itself, or the people we found within it
